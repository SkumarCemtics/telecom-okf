# Subdirectories

* [tables](tables/index.md) - Contains customer analytics tables including customer profiles, churn indicators, network experience, interests, and product recommendation data.

* [datasets](datasets/index.md) - Contains business-oriented analytical concepts for customer segmentation, customer experience, churn analysis, retention management, recommendation analytics, and usage behavior analysis.

* [references](references/index.md) - Contains customer metric definitions, business relationships, customer attributes, and recommended join paths for customer analytics.
