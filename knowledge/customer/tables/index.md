## PostgreSQL Table

- [cem_profile](cem_profile.md)
  - Customer profile information including demographics, customer category, device characteristics, usage patterns, mobility behavior, and customer value indicators.

- [cem_churn](cem_churn.md)
  - Customer churn and retention information including churn drivers, customer experience indicators, recharge behavior, and churn propensity metrics.

- [cem_etwork_exp](cem_etwork_exp.md)
  - Customer-level network experience measurements covering latency, packet loss, coverage, availability, quality, capacity, call experience, and service impact analysis.

- [cem_interest](cem_interest.md)
  - Customer interest and lifestyle segmentation information used for behavioral analytics and personalized engagement.

- [cem_product_recommendation](cem_product_recommendation.md)
  - Product and offer recommendation data used for upsell, cross-sell, retention, and customer targeting analytics.

- [cell_kpi_5g](cell_kpi_5g.md)
  - Cell-level NR (5G) network performance KPIs used to correlate customer experience with underlying network conditions.
