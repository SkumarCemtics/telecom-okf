- [coverage_analysis](coverage_analysis.md) - Business view for coverage, signal quality, mobility, handover performance, and service continuity analysis.

- [customer_experience](customer_experience.md) - Business view for analyzing customer complaints, service quality issues, network experience, and customer impact.

- [outage_management](outage_management.md) - Business view for outage monitoring, service availability analysis, outage impact assessment, and operational performance tracking.

- [root_cause_analysis](root_cause_analysis.md) - Business view for identifying, analyzing, and tracking network issues, root causes, corrective actions, and resolution effectiveness.
